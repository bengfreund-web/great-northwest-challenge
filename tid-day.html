USA Rugby Talent ID Day

A USA Rugby Talent Identification Day for players 16 and older competing in the GNC.

What is a TiD Day?

USA Rugby's Talent Identification Day is used to identify players 16 and older into the USA Rugby Junior National Team pathways.

Players are measured and weighed at check in, and complete physical testing in the form of a 10m and 40m sprint and a standing broad jump. From there, discretionary skill or unit sessions may be run later in the day.

This TiD Day is reserved for players competing in the GNC only.

Schedule

The TiD Day is held at Bozeman Sports Park, 4600 Baxter Ln, Bozeman, MT 59718.

  • Girls: 12:30pm - 2:30pm (12:00pm check in)
  • Boys: 2:30pm - 4:30pm (4:00pm check in)

Registration forms for boys and girls will be posted here ahead of the event.
