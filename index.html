FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enter?

State and regional representative programs (SYROs) in three brackets: High School Boys, High School Girls and U16 Boys, each with a Division 1 and Division 2. This is a high school tournament only, in line with USA Youth and High School Rugby eligibility.

What is the format?

XVs across two days at Montana State University. Matches are 22.5-minute halves with a 5-minute half time, and every team is guaranteed four matches.

What are the divisions?

Six: HS Boys D1, HS Boys D2, HS Girls D1, HS Girls D2, U16 Boys D1 and U16 Boys D2.

What does it cost?

$1,450 per team for both days of competition. Travel, lodging and meals are not included.

What are the age bands?

High School follows USA Youth and High School Rugby eligibility. U16 players must not have reached their 16th birthday by the September 1st that starts the competitive season, including the summer following that school year.

Is there a college showcase?

Yes. The GNC is a College Showcase - credentialed college and university recruiters attend, receive VEO match footage, and access a player recruiting database that athletes can opt into.

What is the USA Rugby Talent ID Day?

USA Rugby runs a Talent Identification (TiD) Day to identify players 16 and older for the Junior National Team pathways. Players are measured, complete physical testing, and may take part in skill sessions. The TiD Day is reserved for players competing in the GNC.

Where is it played?

Montana State University in Bozeman, Montana - four full sized grass pitches plus a purpose built turf rugby field.

How do we get there?

Fly into Bozeman Yellowstone International (BZN), a short drive from the venue. School bus rentals are available through First Student, Inc.

How do we watch?

Every match is livestreamed on the Montana Institute of Sport YouTube channel using VEO cameras.

Can we referee?

Yes. All expenses are covered on arrival and a tournament jersey, shorts and socks are provided. Apply through the referee page.
