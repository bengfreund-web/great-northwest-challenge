Housing & Dining

The Great Northwest Challenge is a Stay and Play event at Montana State University. Teams stay in the campus dorms and eat in the university dining hall, a short walk from the game fields.

Staying and eating on campus keeps player cost down and keeps your whole travelling party together for the weekend. Housing, dining and bookable team spaces are all arranged through Montana State University. The College Expo is Friday, June 25, 6:00–8:00pm, with tournament play June 26 and 27.

Main MSU contact: Hailey Blachly, MSU Student Wellness Center / Campus Rec · hailey.blachly@montana.edu
