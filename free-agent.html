Free Agent Players

No team to play for? Register as a free agent and teams that need players will be in touch.

Haven't got a team to play for but interested in playing with another team? Complete the form below and teams that need players will be in touch.

To be picked up as a free agent, players must:

  • Travel to Bozeman, MT for competition days Saturday June 26th and Sunday June 27th (Friday is optional but encouraged).
  • Currently be USA Rugby registered to a USA Youth and High School rugby club, having competed this season at this level.
  • Be age eligible for their division.
