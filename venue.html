Montana State University

Nestled in beautiful Bozeman, MT and a short drive from Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport, Montana State University offers four full sized grass rugby pitches and a state of the art turf field purpose built for rugby. Our central location allows for easy access to hotels, dining and the major roads out to the stunning scenery in the Bozeman area.
