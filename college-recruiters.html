College Recruiters

Connect with the best high school rugby talent from across the country. Register your college or university below.

The Great Northwest Challenge is a College Showcase. Credentialed college and university recruiters get access to a player recruiting database, a designated recruiting event, and VEO match footage.

  • All registered, credentialed recruiters get access to the recruiting database that players opt into.
  • A college recruiting event is held on Friday, June 25th 2027, with a designated table for each program.
  • VEO links of all match footage are provided to review film.
  • All coaches must hold a credential to access the player recruiting areas; uncredentialed coaches are limited to spectator viewing areas.

$250 per college. Complete the College / University form and make payment to register.
